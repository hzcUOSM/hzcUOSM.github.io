Carrying the Torch of Sports Drama
By cdJapan Originally published in cdJapan

Tetsuya Chiba, the legendary Japanese manga artist best known for "Ashita no Joe." He passed away on October 22, 2026, at the age of 87


Throughout his remarkable 70-year career, Chiba-sensei brought countless memorable characters and stories to life through works including "Ashita no Joe," "Ore wa Teppei," "Notari Matsutaro," and "Ashita Tenki ni Naare."

His expressive artwork and powerful storytelling touched the hearts of generations of readers and creators, leaving a lasting legacy in Japan and around the world. His contributions to manga will continue to be remembered for years to come.


First serialized in 1968, "Ashita no Joe" became one of the defining works of Japanese sports manga. Created by Tetsuya Chiba together with original story writer Asao Takamori (Ikki Kajiwara), the series follows Joe Yabuki, a troubled young man who discovers boxing and struggles to find his place in the world.


Through Joe's journey, his relationship with trainer Danpei Tange, and his unforgettable rivalry with Toru Rikiishi, the manga explores determination, friendship, loss, and the meaning of pursuing one's dreams.


More than half a century after its debut, "Ashita no Joe" remains an enduring part of Japanese manga and anime culture, introducing new generations of readers and viewers to Chiba-sensei's unforgettable characters.
