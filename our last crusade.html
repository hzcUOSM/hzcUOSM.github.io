Our Last Crusade or the Tise of a New World Season 3
By Joanna Cayanan Originally published in Anime News Network

The "Fantasia Bunko Grand Thanksgiving Festival 2026 Online" event on Sunday revealed that a third anime season for Kei Sazane and Ao Nekonabe's Our Last Crusade or the Rise of a New World (Kimi to Boku no Saigo no Senjō, Arui wa Sekai ga Hajimaru Seisen) light novel series is in the works, and also revealed a teaser visual and the returning cast and staff members from the second season.


Yuki Inaba returns to direct the new season at Studio Palette and SILVER LINK., and is also in charge of series scripts. Kaori Yoshikawa is again designing the characters.

The returning cast members are:

  • Yūsuke Kobayashi as Iska
  • Sora Amamiya as Aliceliese Lou Nebulis Ⅸ
  • Nao Shiraki as Mismis Klass
  • Kaori Ishihara as Nene Alkastone
  • Shunichi Toki as Jhin Syulargun
  • Yumiri Hanamori as Rin Vispose
  • Azumi Waki as Sisbell Lou Nebulis Ⅸ

The anime's first season premiered in October 2020. Funimation streamed the anime as it aired in Japan, and it also streamed an English dub.


The second season aired the first four episodes in July 2024, and then delayed episodes from its fifth episode onward in August 2024 "to maintain the quality" of the release. The season resumed airing from the first episode in April 2025. The second season was originally slated to air in 2023 but was delayed to July 2024. Crunchyroll streamed the season as it aired, and it is also streaming an English dub.


Kadokawa began publishing the novels in print in Japan in May 2017, and there are currently 16 volumes in print. Okama launched the manga adaptation in Hakusensha's Young Animal magazine in May 2018, and the manga has a total of seven volumes. The Our Last Crusade or the Rise of a New World: Secret File spinoff light novel series currently has three volumes in print.
