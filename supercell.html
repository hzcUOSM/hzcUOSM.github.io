Supercell Returns
By ManTanWeb Originally published in MANTANWEB

The creative collective "supercell" has announced its relaunch with Yanagi Nagi as its vocalist. It was also announced that their new song, "Yoru to Ao" (Night and Blue), has been chosen as the theme song for the animated film "Mahoutsukai no Yoru" (Witch on the Holy Night).


Yanagi Nagi has provided vocals for numerous supercell songs, including the hit song "Kimi no Shiranai Monogatari," and this marks her first time singing on a new song in approximately 15 years, since their second album "Today Is A Beautiful Day," released on March 16, 2011. supercell also provided the theme song "Hoshi ga Matataku Konna Yoru ni" for the PC game "Mahoutsukai no Yoru," and with Yanagi Nagi on vocals for "Yoru to Ao," they are once again providing the theme song for the same game.

A 90-second video featuring footage from "Mahoutsukai no Yoru" (Witch on the Holy Night) and a snippet of the new song has also been released. Further details, including the song's release information, will be announced at a later date.


supercell's ryo commented, "This is ryo. If it weren't for this opportunity, I think supercell would still be dormant. I'm really happy that we can deliver the continuation of supercell with Nagi's song. Please listen to 'Yoru to Ao'."


Yanagi Nagi said, "Hello everyone! This is Nagi. I'm surprised and happy that in this day and age, I get to sing a new song as supercell again! I remember the day we sang 'Hoshi ga Matataku Konna Yoru ni' as if it were yesterday. Since then, time has passed and ryo's music has evolved even further, and I put my heart into conveying its charm even more. Please listen to 'Yoru to Ao' a lot!"


supercell is a creative collective centered around ryo, with manga artist Shiro Miwa, illustrators redjuice and huke, among others. They depict the world of their works through both music and visuals. In 2007, they gained attention by posting the song "Melt," which uses Hatsune Miku, on Nico Nico Douga, and made their major debut in 2009 with their first album "supercell." The following year, they won the Japan Gold Disc Award for "The Best 5 New Artists." Their representative songs include "Kimi no Shiranai Monogatari," the theme song for the anime "Bakemonogatari," and "My Dearest," the opening theme for "Guilty Crown."


"Mahoutsukai no Yoru" (Witch on the Holy Night) is a theatrical anime film based on a visual novel game by TYPE-MOON, a game brand known for the "Fate" series. The anime is produced by ufotable, known for "Fate/stay night [Heaven's Feel]" and "Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba: Kimetsu no Yaiba". Haruka Tomatsu, Kana Hanazawa as Alice Kuonji, and Yusuke Kobayashi as Soujurou Shizuki, just as they did in the game. It will be released on November 20th.
